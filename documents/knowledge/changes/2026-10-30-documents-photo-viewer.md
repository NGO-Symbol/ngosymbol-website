# Full-screen photo viewer for «Покажіть свої документи…»

## Decision

- Every photo gallery on the autonomous exhibition site opens individual photographs in a shared full-screen viewer, matching the main site's interaction while keeping the exhibition's dossier visual identity (`USER_CONFIRMED`, 2026-10-30).
- The viewer is used by the home exhibition gallery, event galleries and artist work files.
- It supports previous/next controls, keyboard arrows, Escape, captions and credits, focus return, background scroll lock, reduced motion and a mobile control layout.
- While the viewer is open on an artist page, arrow keys navigate photographs only and do not leaf into another artist's personal file.
