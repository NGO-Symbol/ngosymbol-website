# Design system

Built `@symbol/ui` (tokens, tones, motion, `Sym*` components, handbook) and the `apps/design` gallery, adapted from the owner's studio design system. Recorded the decision in ADR-0007 and the agent workflow in the `build-with-design-system` skill and `AGENTS.md`.

Found that the previous fonts have no Cyrillic subset; replaced in the design system by a self-hosted, Cyrillic-capable provisional pair. Found that cream on violet fails body-text contrast; tones encode the rule. Existing site pages are not migrated yet.

Replaced the approximate orbit with the real spiral from the logo SVG, made the mark vector, and added the logo typeface (wordmark and organization name only). Checked the font: Ukrainian letters including «ю» are present; Ъ Ы Э Ё № ₴ are not.
