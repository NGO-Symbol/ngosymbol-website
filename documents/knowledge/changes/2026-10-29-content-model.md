Recorded the site structure agreed on 2026-10-29: six linked record types, a main-site page for every project (ADR-0008, partially superseding ADR-0001), events tied to projects, `single` projects that are also events, year chronology on the projects page, people pages with backlinks, open calls on Home, a join-and-support page, and a logo intro animation.
