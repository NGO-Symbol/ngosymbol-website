## Storage and rights

Most source material is stored in a private Google Drive accessible only to staff. The organization reports that publication permissions exist. Agents must not commit private Drive links, credentials, or unreviewed source dumps. `USER_CONFIRMED`

## Per-project intake checklist

- Confirm current title, dates, status, geography, and direction.
- Confirm Symbol’s exact role.
- Confirm partners/donors and logo-use permission.
- Select hero and gallery media with alt text and credits.
- Confirm team names and roles.
- Confirm outcomes and any numeric claims.
- Collect press links and downloadable files.
- Decide project web type: small page, extended internal section, or autonomous subdomain.
- Define Ukrainian and English publication readiness.
