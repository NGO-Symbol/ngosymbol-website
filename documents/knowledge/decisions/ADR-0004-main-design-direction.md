# ADR-0004: Main-site design direction

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10

## Decision

Use the warm composition, logo-derived colors, and spiral framing of “Orbit” together with the editorial serif typography character of “Index”. Preserve the original logo and its exact dominant colors `#9280A6` and `#FDD08F`.

## Consequences

The institutional site feels contemporary and cultural without losing clarity. Autonomous project sites may diverge while retaining a compact institutional backlink.
