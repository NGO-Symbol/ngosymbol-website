# Website strategy

## Purpose

The site must be informative and must convincingly present the organization to potential donors and partners. It should also route project audiences to relevant experiences, materials, events, and open calls. `USER_CONFIRMED`

## Priority audiences and desired actions

1. **Donors and patrons** — understand capacity and want to fund the organization.
2. **Project audiences** — explore the current project, discover other Symbol work, and read relevant educational material inside that project.
3. **Artists and beneficiaries** — apply to open calls and remember the organization.
4. **Partner institutions** — recognize relevant experience and retain Symbol as a potential collaborator.

Donations, membership, volunteering, and newsletter conversion are possible later but are not MVP requirements.

## MVP

- Institutional home.
- Project gallery with filtering by direction, year, geography, and status.
- Focused pages for small projects.
- Direct cards/links to autonomous project subdomains.
- Events and opportunities.
- About, permanent team, partners, selected policies/reports.
- Contact and partnership route.
- Ukrainian primary version and an English version.
- Search-engine fundamentals and stable QR-safe URLs.

There is no global “Materials / Stories” navigation item. Educational and documentary material belongs inside its relevant project until the organization has enough cross-project material to justify a separate library. `USER_CONFIRMED`

## Content operating model

The organization’s staff own the content. Юрій expects to update the site approximately monthly. Source assets are mostly in a private Google Drive and should be copied into the repository only when cleared for publication. `USER_CONFIRMED`

No CMS is planned. Editors working on the site must be comfortable editing project files or use agent-assisted pull requests. `USER_CONFIRMED`
